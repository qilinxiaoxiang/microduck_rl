About the cameraHead USB camera (Innomaker U20CAM, 1280x720 MJPEG). The A7Z keeps 1 frame in 3, scales it to 640x360 and sends it as JPEG, about 8 fps. The camera starts when the A7Z boots. Tapping "Camera: on" turns it off; tap again to turn it on, and a camera started this way stops by itself once every page watching it is closed. The A7Z rotates the picture 180° (the camera is mounted upside down). Stabilization runs on this Mac: it tracks feature points to get each frame's rotation and shift, turns the frame back along a smoothed path and zooms 1.15x to hide the edges. Slow head turns are followed, fast shake is cancelled. Motion blur within a frame cannot be removed.

Commands go into the control file of walk_wired.py on the A7Z. Walking is hold-to-walk: the page renews the command every 0.15 s, and the duck stops by itself within 1.5 s after you let go, switch away or lose the link. Kick, sit and bend use Pollen's official policies and start only from standing or walking.

Power

Stops the programs on the A7Z, flushes the disk, then shuts the system down. It says the power can be cut only after the A7Z stops responding.

    Press a copper pad on top of the head and its bar rises. The number is the raw XIAO reading, next to it the rise since the last zero. Without the TWI0 overlay the program reads the pins in software, and it pauses while the brain runs on the A7Z.

    Latest readings

    Connection and restart log

      One health reading every 0.5 s; no new data for 1.5 s counts as a lost link. On the A7Z only sysfs and the brain log are read. While the brain is not running, the idle reader (duck_sense.py) reads only servo positions, voltage and the IMU; it gives up the bus as soon as the brain starts, and does not read while the brain runs on a Mac.
