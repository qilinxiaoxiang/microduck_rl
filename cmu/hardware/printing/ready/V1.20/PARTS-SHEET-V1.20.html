MicroDuck prints V1.20

3 parts to print, and 3 printed parts they replace. Every other printed part stays.

V1.20 is one orange plate. It holds the battery backpack frame with a wider switch hole for the Swann 39 switch, the backpack cap, and the rear converter box cap from V1.19, which was never printed. Print this instead of V1.19.

Print these 3 parts

One plate per colour. Each colour has a Bambu X1C file and an Elegoo Centauri Carbon file; use either printer.

Orange plate · 3 parts

Box cap, 3 mm screw floors

Replaces the V1.17 box cap. Same outside and the same four M2 x 10 through the round holes in its back; each head now sits on a 3.0 mm floor (was 1.0 mm). The floors grow toward the boards, so the screw depth is unchanged. Tighten until the cap sits on the rim, then stop. Prints on its flat back face, no brim. 79 × 64 × 15 mm

Backpack frame, Swann hole

Replaces the V1.18 backpack frame. The floor hole is now 12.3 x 27.5 mm, the Swann 39 cutout, and the rocker pocket under it 14.4 mm wide for the 13.8 mm flange; the box is 0.7 mm deeper so the battery clears the thicker switch body. Wire the switch lead first, then push the switch up through the hole from the pocket until both wings click. Prints on its open back rim with tree support and no brim; take every support out from under the front plate and round the lips. 87 × 64 × 22 mm

Battery backpack cap, 3 mm screw floors

Replaces the V1.18 backpack cap. Same outside, 0.7 mm further back to meet the deeper frame; each of the three screw heads sits on a 3.0 mm floor, so the screws are 3 x M2 x 8 (were M2 x 6). Prints on its flat back face, no brim. 77 × 64 × 7 mm
