## What the first review caught

The original 18-case rehearsal passed but missed two real software defects: new wrong-person frames could leave an expired selected-target approach active; and a buffered fetch request could begin ball search before the robot had looked at the caller. Both were independently reproduced, fixed, added to the rehearsal and retested through the original entrypoint. A research review also corrected the description of the old pickup objective: it already included rising, so the new comparison is explicit stable loaded exit/downstream takeover, not “bite versus rise.” Initial rejection evidence is retained in the owning robotics evidence directory.
