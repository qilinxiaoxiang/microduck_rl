# MicroDuck model status — 2026-11-04

The new battery-backpack scans are received and archived in [scans/2026-11-04](scans/2026-11-04/). They record the robot's supplied shape on this date. The new `battery-20261104` articulated model carries an updated engineering mass estimate for this assembly; scan registration and physical qualification remain separate. This update advances P5/P6 (physical-body fidelity and dependable transfer) by preserving the new geometry and making the model gaps explicit.

Start with [load, verify and train the dated model](README.md#load-the-dated-model). No private configuration or credentials are needed for the CPU model check.

## Shape reference and training baseline

Use the new scan package to inspect the battery configuration and compare geometry. Shawn confirms the two scans were taken with power on and closed-loop control, in standing and bent poses. A scan captures visible surfaces in one pose; it does not supply joint axes, component masses, inertias, hidden clearances, or a validated collision model. Use the new dated battery variant for the updated estimate and keep the legacy variants for reproducing earlier experiments. Loading a model is not physical qualification.

The [battery mass variant](variants/battery.json) still describes the 2026-10-15 backpack. Its frame, cap, battery position, plugs and wiring are estimates from that design. The [visual fitted-parts list](fitted.json), checked on 2026-10-26, also predates the current head and backpack assembly. Neither file should be read as a fresh inventory simply because it is named “battery” or “fitted.”

The latest recorded physical battery configuration uses the V1.40 frame and V1.41 cap/hatch. The hatch failed its retention test and is held with foam tape; the printed latch is not accepted. The new scans add shape evidence, not a successful latch test. The Dual Lock attachment had been planned, but its completed fitting was not confirmed in the state record reviewed for this update.

Current physical facts are owned by `robotics/microduck-sim/CURRENT-STATE.json`, especially `battery_backpack_design`, `backpack_swap_plugs`, `parts_a7z_extensions`, and `as_built_scan_readiness`. These are source-checkout references, not files bundled into this public repository. Current sensor timing, supply and deployed policy must be read there before a new robot comparison; historical experiment settings below are not substitutes.

## Component reconciliation

- **Battery enclosure and mounting:** [battery-redesign](../hardware/battery-redesign/README.md) and the shared STL files document the earlier battery-on-cap design. Subsequent quick-swap geometry lives in the source checkout at `robotics/microduck-sim/guide/print/battery-quickswap-20261101/` and `guide/print/release-V1.41/`. The dated variant replaces the old frame/cap masses with these current CAD parts, adds the hatch, bars, screws and foam-tape estimate, and retains the unshifted mounting frame because Dual Lock fitting is unconfirmed.
- **Camera and cable:** the physical-state record identifies the U20CAM as the running camera. The legacy visual list excludes `camera_u20cam` and uses older head shell files. The dated variant replaces the old camera mass placeholder with a CAD-based U20CAM estimate and accounts for the full existing 1 m cable plus an adapter. Cable distribution and adapter position remain explicit assumptions; ordered replacements are not installed parts.
- **Head microphones and touch board:** the 2026-11-01 as-built study includes three INMP441 microphones, the XIAO touch board, foam/glue and their wiring. The legacy visual list excludes `mic_*` and `touch_*`, and the shared battery mass variant does not include these study additions. Their prior engineering mass estimates are now included in the dated variant; no new weighing is required to use this estimated baseline.
- **A7Z saddle and cooling:** the state record identifies a V1.35 saddle with no lid and infers the 6530B heatsink from that assembly. The legacy visual list instead pins the D-022 saddle body and board pose. Resolve the actual orientation and visible parts against the scan; do not silently substitute the newest designed saddle or an unfitted lid.
- **Ankles:** [apply_masses.py](apply_masses.py) already applies the 1.36 rad heel stop to the CMU model routes, checked by [test_ankle_stop.py](test_ankle_stop.py). This is a model constraint. Older Dock experiments used a different ankle assumption, so their results cannot be transferred to this baseline without reconciling it.

A useful intermediate source is `robotics/microduck-sim/guide/firmware/mac/namecall-20261029/glance-20261101/variant-asbuilt-20261101.json`: it adds the earlier battery-on-cap position, cradle/bars, top plugs and head sensors to the legacy battery variant. It does not incorporate every later enclosure change, and its mass estimates are not new measurements.

## Updated engineering mass estimate

[variants/battery-20261104.json](variants/battery-20261104.json) derives from the legacy battery variant without modifying it. [The generated manifest](manifests/battery-20261104.json) contains 225 unique signed-mass entries. [Mass provenance](battery-20261104-mass-provenance.json) records the source mesh hashes, volumes, centres and tensors.

Printed parts use the prior method: CAD volume × 1.24 g/cm³ × 0.851 effective printed fraction. The V1.40 frame is estimated at 16.197 g, V1.41 cap at 9.761 g, hatch at 1.774 g, and both bars together at 0.642 g. The cradle is already inside the cap mesh and is not added again. The current ear cap, lower shell, jaw support/frame, face, trim and jaw ring also replace their legacy mass entries using their pinned geometry. The retired speaker retainer and amplifier-post screws are removed; the speaker/amplifier and microphone/touch estimates use current component positions. Existing battery, plug and wiring estimates are retained or redistributed; the old camera placeholder and cable lump are replaced.

The compiled model totals **960.45 g**, an estimated **30.31 g** above the legacy battery model. At its HOME pose, the calculated centre of mass moves **0.20 mm rearward and 1.78 mm upward**. These are model calculations, not scale measurements or scan measurements. Both models compile with positive moving-body masses and inertias. Density, camera composition, cable allocation and tape/adapter mass ranges are recorded explicitly for later sensitivity checks; this update does not require the user to reweigh the parts.

## Latest recorded training work

The latest training entry located in `robotics/docs/PET_ROBOT_LOG.md` is dated **2026-11-03 12:50**. Dock-e and Dock-f each completed 3,000 iterations. Their training docking metric plateaued around 17–23% from 5 cm starts; the metric requires the ball near the pickup spot while the robot is still and upright. This is a training statistic, not an independent pickup or fetch success rate. Work was paused at Shawn's request.

Independent checkpoint evaluation, inspection of failed approaches, and explanation of the oracle's incomplete score remain open. The Dock source was recorded in a separate deployment-machine checkout; its canonical source/package reconciliation is also open. The presence of checkpoints does not mean that Dock has been merged into this public training baseline or accepted on the robot.

Earlier T2 pixel-pick experiments showed useful local visual control but did not meet the full starting-position acceptance target. Their training-environment results and the separate as-built replay results have different model provenance and must remain distinguishable. Evidence is in the source checkout's `microduck_rl-grasp/cmu/grasp/T2-PIXEL.md` and `guide/firmware/mac/t2-chain-20261101/README.md`.

## What changes the readiness decision

The new scan closes “scan not received,” and the dated variant provides an executable estimate based on the existing CAD/component method. Qualification for transfer to this robot still requires geometric registration of the supplied powered poses, comparison with synchronized joint/IMU samples where available, and baseline plus perturbation checks. Label experiments with the exact model and manifest revision. No new training run or physical acceptance is claimed by this update.
